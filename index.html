How to Play 65536

Welcome to 65536 — the tactical reverse of 2048!

🚫 NO MERGING: Tiles never merge together. Two numbers touching do not combine!

🔨 Tactical Power-ups:

🔨 Shatter Hammer (≤ 256 only): Starts with 1 hammer. Earn +1 on every Level win! Can only shatter tiles with value ≤ 256.
⚡ Breaker Warp (200,000 Pts): Earn +1 Warp charge every 200,000 points! Tap Warp and click any empty cell to place the breaker wherever you need it!

⚡ Tile Breakers:

÷2 Crimson Breaker (75%): Divides tile by 2 into 2 pieces
÷4 Gold Breaker (15%): Divides tile by 4 into 4 pieces (cross formation)
÷8 Cyan Breaker (10% BONUS!): Detonates tile into 8 surrounding pieces (radial burst), pushing nearby tiles outward!

There is only one breaker tile at a time on the board coming randomly as in 2048, so the board never clutters with unused breakers!

✨ Clear to Win: When any target tile is reduced to 16, 8, 4, or 2 (≤ 16), it shatters and disappears completely from the board!

Ultimate Goal: Clear all target tiles to advance through the campaign from 4096 all the way to 65536!
