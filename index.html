How to Play 65536

Welcome to 65536 — the tactical reverse of 2048!

🚫 NO MERGING: Tiles never merge together. Two numbers touching do not combine!

🔨 Tactical Power-ups:

🔨 Shatter Hammer (≤ 256 only): Starts with 1 hammer. Earn +1 hammer every 25,000 points (and +1 on every Level win)! Can only shatter tiles with value ≤ 256.
⚡ Breaker Warp (50,000 Pts): Starts with 1 Warp. Earn +1 Warp charge every 50,000 points! Tap Warp and click any empty cell to place the breaker wherever you need it!

⚡ Tile Breakers:

÷2 Crimson Breaker (75%): Divides tile by 2 into 2 pieces
÷4 Gold Breaker (15%): Divides tile by 4 into 4 pieces (cross formation)
÷8 Cyan Breaker (10% BONUS!): Detonates tile into 8 pieces with Carrom Board Kinetic Scatter! Pieces shoot outward along open momentum lanes powered purely by breaker striking force, dispersing all tiles (regardless of value) equally across the board!

There is only one breaker tile at a time on the board coming randomly as in 2048, so the board never clutters with unused breakers!

✨ Clear to Win: When any target tile is reduced to 16, 8, 4, or 2 (≤ 16), it shatters and disappears completely from the board!
💥 Supernova / Big Bang Finishing: When remaining tiles on the board can mathematically never overflow the grid (even under 8-dividers), a critical mass cascade triggers automatically! All tiles detonate from smallest to largest in an epic escalating Big Bang explosion with full bonus points awarded. Watch the live 💥 Big Bang status box right above the board to track remaining tiles!
🎬 Move-History Replay: Tap the 🎬 header button anytime to review, play back (0.5x–4x), scrub through your game timeline, or export/import complete games as JSON!

Ultimate Goal: Clear all target tiles to advance through the campaign from 4096 all the way to 65536!
